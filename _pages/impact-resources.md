Public comment periods, consultations, and committee submissions are real levers, many of which take feedback not only from citizens and immigrants, but also from private companies and subject matter experts. Most people just don't know where to find them.

This page provides my favourite official channels and starting templates for the issues I follow most closely. Pick your country, then the topic closest to what you want to weigh in on.

<p style="color: var(--color-ink-soft); font-size: var(--fs-sm);"><strong>Disclose your stake.</strong> Wherever you are, or are immigrating to, state your "stakeholder status" as well as any conflicts of interest when you comment. As the same time, in most cases you can ask personal details be redacted from the record, including your name and contact information.</p>

<p style="color: var(--color-ink-soft); font-size: var(--fs-sm);"><strong>These tools work.</strong> They are real levers outside of electoral processes to be used with care. As an international undergraduate, I used my stakeholder status to flag medical inadmissibility policies that fundamentally conflicted with Canadian human rights. Within a month of my reporting this gap as a mere junior non-citizen, the federal government issued an emergency change affecting real immigration cases. <em>Be careful what you wish for because they just might implement it.</em></p>

  <h2 class="section__title">Canada</h2>

  <div class="cards">
    <div class="card">
      <h3 class="card__title">Government consultations</h3>
      <p class="card__body">The Canada Gazette is the official newspaper of the Government of Canada. Learn about and give feedback on new and proposed policies in Part I, typically 30&ndash;75 days before they take effect. Canada.ca also indexes every open federal consultation, including calls on AI and data governance (AIDA).</p>
      <p style="display:flex;flex-wrap:wrap;gap:var(--sp-3);margin-top:var(--sp-4)">

        <a class="btn" href="https://www.geds-sage.gc.ca/en/GEDS/?pgid=012">Federal Contact List &rarr;</a>
        <a class="btn" href="https://gazette.gc.ca/accueil-home-eng.html">Canada Gazette &rarr;</a>
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3 class="card__title">Parliamentary committees</h3>
      <p class="card__body">Standing committees studying a bill accept written briefs from the
      public, usually with a posted deadline tied to the study schedule. Briefs should stay under
      10 pages, or include a short summary if longer.</p>
      <p style="display:flex;flex-wrap:wrap;gap:var(--sp-3);margin-top:var(--sp-4)">
        <a class="btn btn--primary" href="https://www.ourcommons.ca/procedure/guides/brief-e.html">Write Policy Briefs &rarr;</a>
        <a class="btn" href="https://www.ourcommons.ca/procedure/our-procedure/index-e.html">How Parliament Works &rarr;</a>
        <a class="btn" href="https://www.ourcommons.ca/Committees/en/Home">Find a Committee &rarr;</a>
      </p>
    </div>
  </div>
