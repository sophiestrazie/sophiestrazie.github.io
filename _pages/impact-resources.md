Public comment periods, consultations, and committee submissions are real levers, most people
just don't know where to find them. This page collects the official channels and starting
templates for the issues I follow most closely. Pick your country, then the topic closest to
what you want to weigh in on.

<section class="section">
  <p class="section__eyebrow">Canada</p>
  <h2 class="section__title">Canada</h2>

  <div class="cards">
    <div class="card">
      <h3 class="card__title">Federal regulations</h3>
      <p class="card__body">Proposed regulations are posted for public comment in the Canada
      Gazette, Part I, typically for 30&ndash;75 days before they take effect.</p>
      <p><a class="btn" href="https://gazette.gc.ca/accueil-home-eng.html">Canada Gazette &rarr;</a></p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3 class="card__title">Parliamentary committees</h3>
      <p class="card__body">Standing committees studying a bill accept written briefs from the
      public, usually with a posted deadline tied to the study schedule.</p>
      <p>
        <a class="btn" href="https://www.ourcommons.ca/Committees/en/Home">Committee submissions &rarr;</a>
        &nbsp;<a class="btn btn--primary" href="[link to brief template]">Brief template</a>
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3 class="card__title">AI &amp; digital policy</h3>
      <p class="card__body">Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED) and the
      Office of the Privacy Commissioner periodically open consultations on AI and data
      governance, including the AI and Data Act (AIDA) process.</p>
      <p><a class="btn" href="https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/consultations/en">ISED consultations &rarr;</a></p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <p class="section__eyebrow">United States</p>
  <h2 class="section__title">United States</h2>

  <div class="cards">
    <div class="card">
      <h3 class="card__title">Federal rulemaking</h3>
      <p class="card__body">Agencies must post proposed rules for public comment under the
      Administrative Procedure Act. Every open docket is searchable by agency or keyword.</p>
      <p><a class="btn" href="https://www.regulations.gov/">Regulations.gov &rarr;</a></p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3 class="card__title">Congressional input</h3>
      <p class="card__body">Representatives and senators track constituent messages on specific
      bills. A short, specific message outweighs a form letter.</p>
      <p>
        <a class="btn" href="https://www.house.gov/representatives/find-your-representative">Find your rep &rarr;</a>
        &nbsp;<a class="btn btn--primary" href="[link to letter template]">Letter template</a>
      </p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <p class="section__eyebrow">European Union</p>
  <h2 class="section__title">European Union</h2>

  <div class="cards">
    <div class="card">
      <h3 class="card__title">Commission consultations</h3>
      <p class="card__body">The "Have Your Say" portal lists every open call for evidence and
      public consultation across Commission departments, with submission deadlines.</p>
      <p><a class="btn" href="https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/better-regulation/have-your-say_en">Have Your Say &rarr;</a></p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3 class="card__title">European Parliament</h3>
      <p class="card__body">Committees issue calls for evidence while drafting reports, and any
      EU citizen or resident can file a petition on a matter within EU competence.</p>
      <p><a class="btn" href="https://petiport.secure.europarl.europa.eu/petitions/en/home">Petitions portal &rarr;</a></p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
